Holy Mode

Block apps, whole categories and websites with Apple's own Screen Time, and turn it on from any screen. When the shield stops you, HolyMode walks you through a guided breath and a passage of Scripture until the moment passes without taking you with it.

A streak worth keeping

One number, counted honestly, with a record of the days you stood firm and the days you did not. A setback is written down and forgiven, not hidden.

Thirty shields

Days stood add up across your whole walk, not only since your last setback, and they earn shields — thirty of them, from your first day to your thousandth. One hard evening never takes back what you have already earned.

The Bible, built in

Three complete translations: the King James, the Douay-Rheims and the World English Bible. A reader set to your choice of type and size, and a passage for every day.

Your progress stays on your iPhone

Your streak, your notes and the apps you chose to block never leave the device. Screen Time hands HolyMode anonymous tokens rather than names, so not even the app can see what you picked. No ads. No trackers. No data brokers.
